Peacepot Support

Help with the Peacepot app for iOS.

Contact us

For questions, feedback, or issues with the app, email us at wildsproutstudios.support@gmail.com.

Reporting a problem

Please email us and include what you were doing when the issue occurred, the steps to reproduce it if you can, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. A screenshot helps a lot. We read every message.

Frequently asked

  • Do I need an account? No. Peacepot works without an account or sign-in.
  • Where is my data stored? Your income and settings are stored only on your device. We don't receive or store them on any server. Deleting the app removes this data, so note your numbers first if you want to keep them.
  • How do the percentages work? Peacepot divides your income into Needs, Buffer, Savings, Debt, and Fun. You can pick a starting point (Student, Single, Couple, or Family) and then move each share a few points up or down in Settings. Savings, Debt, and Fun can be switched off. Needs and Buffer are always on. Shares are scaled so the total always adds up to 100%.
  • How do I remove ads? Tap "Remove ads" above the banner, or open Settings and choose "Go ad-free". It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads, or I have a new phone. Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchase". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.
  • Can I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • I want to change my ad or consent choices. Email us and we'll help. You can also limit tracking per app in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Is Peacepot financial advice? No. Peacepot gives general budgeting guidance based on fixed percentages. Please see the Terms of Service for details.
  • Which languages are supported? English and Dutch.
