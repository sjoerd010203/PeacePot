Privacy Policy

This policy describes what information Peacepot collects, how it's used, and your choices. Peacepot is provided by Sjoerd van Lexmond (WildSprout Studios), who is responsible for the processing described here.

In short: Peacepot has no accounts. The income and budget settings you enter stay on your device. We don't run servers that store your data. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob.

Information We Collect

  • Information you enter in the app: your monthly income, your chosen budget percentages, and your settings. This is stored locally on your device. We do not receive it and cannot see it.
  • Purchase information: if you buy "Remove ads", the payment is handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether you own the purchase.
  • Advertising and device data: the free version of Peacepot shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information such as your device's IP address, device and operating system details, advertising identifiers, and how you interact with ads, in order to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance, and prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), ads are only requested after you have made your choice in a consent message.
  • Messages you send us: if you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.
  • Apple analytics: if you have chosen to share app analytics with developers in iOS, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics. We don't receive information that identifies you.

How We Use Information

  • To provide the app's functionality, which runs on your device.
  • To show ads that help fund the free version, and to remove them once you buy "Remove ads".
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob serves the ads. Google acts under its own privacy practices. Read more in the Google Privacy Policy and in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Google may process data outside the European Economic Area.
  • Apple provides the App Store, handles payments for "Remove ads", and manages your purchase history under your Apple ID.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Information is shared only with the services above (Google for ads, Apple for purchases), and only as described in this policy.

Consent and Your Choices

  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Peacepot shows a consent message before requesting ads. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us.
  • You can limit tracking for individual apps in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can remove ads entirely with the one-time "Remove ads" purchase.
  • You can delete everything Peacepot stores about you by deleting the app from your device. We hold no copy of this data.

Data Retention

Your income and settings stay on your device until you delete the app or reset it. Emails you send to support are kept only as long as needed to help you and to meet legal obligations.

Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or move your personal data, to object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold almost no personal data about you, most of this is handled by deleting the app, but you can contact us at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority, which in the Netherlands is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
